## 🌟 Overview

IntelliPath AI is a comprehensive AI-powered career development platform that helps students, graduates, and professionals discover, plan, and accelerate their ideal career paths.

Unlike traditional career guidance platforms that provide generic suggestions, IntelliPath uses advanced AI models, industry intelligence, personalized assessments, and workflow automation to generate tailored career recommendations, learning roadmaps, resume reviews, interview preparation plans, and skill development strategies.

The platform functions as a 24/7 AI Career Mentor capable of helping users make informed professional decisions backed by real-world market trends and intelligent recommendations.
